Our Programmes

What we do every month in Gauteng schools. We have 3 main programmes.




1. Monthly Packs

We give free sanitary packs to school girls. In each pack we put Bodyform pads, small soap, Nivea deodorant, tissues and a booklet. This is the real photo of our packs (see image above).


Cost per pack: R50 | Girls helped: 5000+



2. Education Workshops

We go to schools in Soweto and Tembisa and teach girls about periods, hygiene and health. We use simple posters and we talk in their language. We did 45 workshops in 2025.


Schools visited: 200 | Free for all schools



3. Community Drives

We partner with local shops in Pretoria and Joburg to collect pads and soap. People can drop donations at our office or we collect. Our volunteers help pack and deliver.


Partners: 12 shops | Volunteers: 25
